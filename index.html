03 / METHOD & BOUNDARIES

A precise exhibit, not a return forecast.

Data

Yahoo Finance monthly adjusted closes for eight public ETF proxies, normalized to 100 in January 2020.

Risk

Growth, monthly return, drawdown and realized volatility are calculated from the downloaded monthly series.

Decision

The current-weight portfolio path is a research index, not an account or a recommendation.

Read method notes

The 3D chart uses 82 monthly observations from January 2020 through October 2026. Cumulative wealth is indexed to 100. The Y axis is independently scaled for each view, while the X axis preserves time order and the Z axis preserves one channel per asset.

The 3D model encodes observed paths, not probabilities. Text labels and the diagnostics remain the authoritative source for values.
