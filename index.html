03 / METHOD & BOUNDARIES

A precise exhibit, not a return forecast.

Data

Public ETF adjusted closes translated to HKD without hedging. Raw vendor history is not redistributed.

Risk

Variance contribution uses the trailing covariance matrix. Stress returns are illustrative historical mappings.

Decision

The equity de-risk control is an interaction example, not an investment recommendation or live trading rule.

Read method notes

Baseline metrics use the L1 TAA research series: 5.20% annual return, 7.64% annual volatility, 0.70 Sharpe ratio and −23.32% maximum drawdown. Scenario losses are weighted asset moves multiplied by the user-selected correlation-stress factor and are displayed for structural comparison only.

The 3D model encodes relationships, not probabilities. Text labels and the diagnostics remain the authoritative source for values.
